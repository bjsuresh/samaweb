SAP Interface

Secure OT-to-ERP Data Integration Platform

Seamlessly transfer validated operational data from the SAMA Historian to SAP and other Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, enabling business applications to operate using trusted production information.

Built on the SAMA Historian

SAMA ERP Integration leverages the SAMA Historian as the enterprise source of operational truth. By consolidating data from multiple industrial systems into a centralized historian, ERP systems receive validated, contextualized and historical process information through a single integration point.

Single Source of Operational Truth
Deliver validated operational data from a centralized historian instead of establishing multiple direct connections to PLCs, DCSs or SCADA systems.
Industry-Agnostic ERP Integration
Support ERP integration across oil & gas, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, power, metals, cement and many other industries.
Flexible Data Delivery
Provide operational data continuously, on-demand or in scheduled batches according to ERP application requirements.
Secure Enterprise Integration
Integrate with SAP and other ERP platforms using approved enterprise APIs while maintaining secure separation between OT and IT.
